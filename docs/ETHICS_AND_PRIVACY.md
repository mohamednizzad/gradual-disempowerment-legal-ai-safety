# Ethics, privacy, and data handling

This repository is a research prototype, not an approved human-subjects protocol.

Before collecting participant data:

1. Obtain any institutional ethics review or determination required for the project.
2. Provide a participant information sheet and informed-consent process.
3. Use fictional, public-domain, or explicitly approved materials. Do not collect privileged or confidential client content.
4. Minimize data collection. Avoid storing raw prompts or drafts unless essential and consented to.
5. Separate contact details from research records and restrict access to authorized researchers.
6. Define a retention and deletion schedule before recruitment.
7. Do not send sensitive research data to external AI services.
8. Do not release participant-level outputs. Public releases should use synthetic data or materials reviewed for confidentiality and re-identification risk.
9. Do not rank named professionals, firms, or individual participants.
10. Report limitations and avoid claims that a proxy metric proves loss of agency.

This checklist is not legal advice and does not replace jurisdiction-specific review.
